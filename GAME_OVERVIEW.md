# Asteroid Shooter - Game Overview

## Description
Asteroid Shooter is a classic arcade-style space shooter where you control a triangular spaceship navigating through an asteroid field. Your objective is to survive as long as possible by destroying incoming asteroids while avoiding collisions. Use arrow keys or WASD to rotate and thrust your ship, and press SPACE to shoot bullets. As your score increases, more asteroids spawn, making the game progressively harder. The game ends when your ship collides with an asteroid, after which you can restart and try to beat your high score.

## Controls
- **Arrow Keys / WASD**: Rotate ship left/right, thrust forward
- **SPACE**: Shoot bullets
- **ESC**: Pause/Return to menu
- **ENTER**: Start game / Restart after game over

## Objectives
- Destroy asteroids to earn points
- Survive as long as possible
- Avoid collisions with asteroids
- Beat your high score
